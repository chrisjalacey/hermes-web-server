Bilbao & Basque Country

Authentic Non-Tourist Guide — Local Experiences, Hidden Gems & Dietary-Friendly Dining

🔑 Key Findings

  • Aste Nagusia (Big Week) runs Aug 22–30, 2026 — after your mid-August trip. No major Bilbao festivals during your stay.
  • San Sebastián's Semana Grande runs Aug 8–15, 2026. Aug 15 is the final day with nightly fireworks over La Concha bay — perfect for a day trip.
  • Aug 15 is a Spanish national holiday (Assumption) — festive buzz; some shops closed, restaurants/festivals open.
  • Gaztelugatxe requires a free timed-entry reservation in high season — sold out for mid-August. Alternative: Cabo Matxitxako headland (5 min drive) offers the iconic aerial view of the islet from the cliffs, zero reservation, fewer crowds, two historic lighthouses, and a stunning coastal loop.
  • Pescatarian-friendly: Basque cuisine is exceptionally fish-forward (bacalao/hake, kokotxas, squid, anchovy) with great vegetable pintxos — no problem at any stop.
  • Gluten-free: Bilbao has dedicated 100% GF venues (Grosso Napoletana, BAUSK, Magora, Kuki) plus many GF-aware spots. FACE (Spanish Celiac Association) certification is the gold standard.
