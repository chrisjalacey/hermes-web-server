🏖️ Beach Guide — Basque Coast & Getaria

Beaches, coves, and coastal spots for mid-August visitors — facilities, transport & practical tips

🔑 Key Insights for Beach-Goers

  • August water temperature: 21–24°C — perfect for swimming; 3/2mm wetsuit for surf.
  • Getaria's Malkorbe Beach: Blue Flag, sandy, calm waters, full facilities, 5 min walk from town centre.
  • Zarautz (15 min away): 2.5–3 km of sand — longest Basque beach, surf capital, big parking but busy in August.
  • Bilbao Metro Line 1: Direct to Las Arenas, Ereaga, Sopelana, Plentzia — best way to avoid August parking chaos.Flysch Coast (Zumaia/Deba): Dramatic vertical rock strata; low tide essential for access.
  • Parking in Getaria: Blue Zone regulated daily 10:00–20:00 (June–Sept); €1.50/hr, €12/day max.
  • Dogs: Prohibited during bathing hours; allowed 21:00–06:00 (Easter–Oct 14); 20:00–09:30 at Sopelana/Barinatxe.

🌊 Beach Culture & August Tips

August Crowds: All beaches busy 11:00–19:00. Arrive before 10:30 for parking at Zarautz/Malkorbe. San Sebastián beaches extremely crowded — consider Getaria/Zarautz instead. Bilbao Metro Line 1 is your best friend for Uribe Kosta beaches.
Typical Beach Hours
11:00–20:00 peak; Spanish families arrive 12:00–13:00, stay till sunset
Nudist Sections
Azkorri (Getxo), Barinatxe left side (Sopelana), Saturraran (Mutriku); signposted
Best Times
Early morning (9:00–11:00) for space; late afternoon (18:00+) for cooler temps
Tide Awareness
Flysch (Zumaia/Deba) & Mundaka rivermouth only at low tide; check tables day before
Parking Strategy
Use Metro Line 1 for Uribe Kosta; arrive before 10:30 or after 19:00 if driving
Golden Hour
20:00–22:00 for surfing/walks when dogs permitted; magical light for photos
