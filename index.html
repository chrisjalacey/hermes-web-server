Bilbao & San Sebastián

August 14 – 19, 2026  |  A Throuple's Getaway

🔑 Key Findings

  • Bilbao's Aste Nagusia (Big Week) runs Aug 22–30, 2026 — after your trip. No major Bilbao festivals during your stay.
  • San Sebastián's Semana Grande runs Aug 8–15, 2026. Aug 15 is the final day with nightly fireworks over the bay. Day trip scheduled for this date to catch the festival.
  • Aug 15 is also a Spanish national holiday (Assumption) — festive buzz; some shops closed, restaurants/festivals open.
  • Gaztelugatxe requires a free timed-entry reservation in high season — sold out for your dates. Alternative: Cabo Matxitxako headland (5 min drive from Gaztelugatxe) offers the iconic aerial view of the islet from the cliffs, zero reservation, fewer crowds, two historic lighthouses, and a stunning coastal loop.
  • Pescatarian-friendly: Basque cuisine is exceptionally fish-forward (bacalao/hake, kokotxas, squid, anchovy) with great vegetable pintxos — no problem at any stop.

🥾 Sun, Aug 16 — Cabo Matxitxako Headland Hike & Bermeo (Gaztelugatxe sold out — better alternative)

🌤️ Weather (Sun 16 Aug): Light cloud, 26°C/79°F high, 18°C/64°F low, NW 10 mph. Hourly forecast available ~7 days out — check BBC Weather closer to date.

  • 09:00 Depart Bilbao by rental car (BI-631 → BI-3101 coastal road, ~45 min, stunning drive).
  • 09:45 Arrive Matxitxako Lighthouse parking (free, ~30 spaces — arrive early to secure spot).
  • 10:00–12:30 Hike the headland:
    • New Lighthouse (1909) — 5 min paved walk from parking, panoramic platform
    • Old Lighthouse (1852) — 15 min coastal trail, ruins + dramatic cliff views
    • Gaztelugatxe Viewpoint — continue 30 min past old lighthouse for the iconic aerial view of Gaztelugatxe islet (no reservation, no crowds)
  • 13:00 Drive 5 min to Bermeo — lunch at Kaia Kai or Boliño (grilled fish, txakoli, harbor views).
  • 15:00 Optional: Ercilla Tower (fisherman's museum) or bakio beach detour on return.
  • 16:30 Return to Bilbao.
