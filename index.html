Bilbao & San Sebastián

August 14 – 19, 2026  |  A Throuple's Getaway

🔑 Key Findings

  • Bilbao's Aste Nagusia (Big Week) runs Aug 22–30, 2026 — after your trip. No major Bilbao festivals during your stay.
  • San Sebastián's Semana Grande runs Aug 8–15, 2026. Aug 15 is the final day with nightly fireworks over the bay. Day trip scheduled for this date to catch the festival.
  • Aug 15 is also a Spanish national holiday (Assumption) — festive buzz; some shops closed, restaurants/festivals open.
  • Gaztelugatxe requires a free timed-entry reservation in high season — sold out for your dates. Alternative: Cabo Matxitxako headland (5 min drive from Gaztelugatxe) offers the iconic aerial view of the islet from the cliffs, zero reservation, fewer crowds, two historic lighthouses, and a stunning coastal loop.
  • Pescatarian-friendly: Basque cuisine is exceptionally fish-forward (bacalao/hake, kokotxas, squid, anchovy) with great vegetable pintxos — no problem at any stop.
