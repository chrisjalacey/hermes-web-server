🌤️ Weather & What to Pack

🌡️ Typical August Weather
  • Temperatures: 18–26°C (64–79°F) — pleasant, not extreme
  • Rain: Oceanic climate — occasional showers likely; carry light rain jacket
  • Humidity: Moderate to high near coast
  • Daylight: Sunrise ~7:00, Sunset ~21:30 (long evenings)
🧳 Packing Essentials
  • Light layers (t-shirts, light sweater/cardigan for evenings)
  • Waterproof shell or compact umbrella
  • Comfortable walking shoes (cobblestones in Casco Viejo)
  • For sidrería: Old shoes (sticky floors), warm layers (cold cellars)
  • For hiking Matxitxako: Sturdy shoes, hat, water, sunscreen
  • Reusable water bottle (tap water excellent)

🎪 Aste Nagusia (Aug 22–30) — If You're Staying Through

Major Festival Impact: If your trip extends into Aste Nagusia, prepare for: 40–60% hotel price increases, massive crowds, street closures, noise until 3-4am, limited restaurant availability.
📅 Booking Timeline
  • Accommodation: Book 3–4 months ahead (by April/May 2026)
  • Restaurants: Book 4–6 weeks ahead for popular spots
  • Txosnas: No booking needed — just show up
🎭 What Happens During Aste Nagusia
  • Txosnas: ~20 pop-up bars along Arenal/Ribera quays run by local clubs (comparsas)
  • Free concerts: Main stage at Plaza Nueva & secondary stages nightly
  • Fireworks: Nightly over estuary (~22:30), best viewed from riverside
  • Marijaia: Festival mascot burned on final day (Aug 30)
  • Basque sports: Daily herri kirolak displays (stone lifting, wood chopping)
  • Parades: Comparsas parade through streets in costumes
🚫 What to Avoid During Festival
  • Driving in city center — streets closed, parking impossible
  • Staying near txosna zone if you need sleep before midnight
  • Expecting quiet dinners — everything is loud and festive
  • Last-minute restaurant bookings — fully booked weeks out

🚌 Transport — Getting Around

🎫 Barik Card (Essential)
  • Rechargeable transit card for Metro, Bilbao Bizkaibus, Euskotren, Dbus (San Sebastián)
  • Buy at Metro stations, tobacco shops (estancos), airport
  • €3 card + credit; discounts vs single tickets
  • Works across all Bizkaia & Gipuzkoa transport
🚇 Metro Bilbao
  • Lines L1, L2, L3 cover Bilbao + Getxo + Barakaldo + Santurtzi
  • Festival extended hours: Runs until 2-3am during Aste Nagusia
  • Key stops: Casco Viejo, Moyúa, Indautxu, San Mamés, Deusto, Guggenheim, Getxo
✈️ Airport (BIO) → City Center
  • Bizkaibus A3247: Every 15–30 min, 25 min to Plaza Moyúa/Gran Vía, €3 with Barik
  • Taxi: ~€25–30, 15–20 min
  • Rental car: All major companies at airport; book ahead for August
🚗 Driving & Parking
  • Avoid city center driving — narrow streets, pedestrian zones, confusing one-way system
  • Park at hotel or peripheral parking (Bilbao Parking app for real-time availability)
  • Day trips: Car recommended for Getaria, Bermeo, Rioja Alavesa, Gorbeia
  • San Sebastián day trip: Park at Illunbe P+R (free, ~1200 spaces) + Dbus Line 28 to center
  • AP-8 toll highway to San Sebastián (~€12) vs N-1 scenic coastal (free, slower)

💡 Reservation Tips
  • Use TheFork (ElTenedor) for English interface & instant confirmation
  • For Michelin: call directly or use restaurant website booking engine
  • Always mention dietary needs (GF/pescatarian) at time of booking
  • Confirm 24h before — Spanish restaurants appreciate reconfirmation
  • Late lunch (13:30–15:00) easier to book than dinner (20:30+)

💰 Money & Payments

💳 Payment Methods
  • Cards accepted almost everywhere (Visa/Mastercard/Amex)
  • Contactless widely available
  • Carry some cash (€50–100) for small pintxo bars, markets, taxis
  • ATMs: plentiful; use bank ATMs (avoid Euronet high fees)
💰 Tipping Culture
  • Not obligatory; 5–10% for excellent service at sit-down restaurants
  • Pintxo bars: round up or leave small change
  • Taxis: round up to nearest euro
  • No tip for counter service/coffee

🛡️ Safety & Practical

🎒 Pickpocket Prevention
  • High risk in: Casco Viejo crowds, Metro (peak hours), txosna zones, festival areas
  • Use front-pocket wallet, money belt, or crossbody bag with zipper
  • Don't leave phones/wallets on restaurant tables
  • Be wary of distraction techniques (maps, petitions, "found" items)
🏥 Health & Pharmacy
  • Pharmacies (farmacia) everywhere — green cross sign
  • 24h pharmacy: Farmacia Goikoetxea (C/ Gran Vía 80), Farmacia Zabala (C/ Alameda Recalde 14)
  • EU health card (EHIC/GHIC) covers public healthcare
  • Tap water: excellent quality, drink freely
📱 Useful Apps
  • Moovit / Google Maps — public transport
  • Bilbao Parking — real-time parking availability
  • TheFork (ElTenedor) — restaurant reservations
  • Dbus — San Sebastián city bus (for Aug 15 trip)
  • Euskotren / Bizkaibus — regional train/bus
  • FACE Móvil — gluten-free restaurant finder (Spain celiac association)
