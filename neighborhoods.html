🗺️ Neighborhood Guide

Where locals actually live, eat, and hang out — beyond the tourist zones

Bilbao Neighborhoods

Casco Viejo (Old Town)
Metro
Casco Viejo (L1, L2, L3)
Vibe
Historic, lively, pintxo-dense Touristy Local Favorite

What to do: Wander the Siete Calles (Seven Streets), visit Ribera Market (Europe's largest covered market), Plaza Nueva for pintxos, Cathedral of Santiago.

Key restaurants: AHOAN, Gure Toki, Café Bar Bilbao, Irrintzi, La Viña del Ensanche (nearby).

Best for: Pintxo crawl, morning market visit, historic atmosphere.

Ensanche / Indautxu
Metro
Moyúa (L1, L2), Indautxu (L1)
Vibe
Upscale, commercial, wide avenues Local Favorite

What to do: Shop Gran Vía, visit Azkuna Zentroa (cultural center), Museo de Bellas Artes, stroll Doña Casilda park.

Key restaurants: La Viña del Ensanche, Kimtxu, Grosso Napoletana, Etxanobe Atelier (in Azkuna Zentroa), BAUSK.

Best for: Upscale dining, cultural venues, quieter accommodation during Aste Nagusia.

Abandoibarra
Metro
Guggenheim (L1), Abando (L1, L2)
Vibe
Modern, riverside, cultural Local Favorite

What to do: Guggenheim Museum, Iberdrola Tower, riverside promenade, Zubizuri bridge, Euskalduna Palace.

Key restaurants: Kasko, Nerua Guggenheim (⭐), rooftop bars.

Best for: Modern architecture, museums, riverside walks.

Santutxu
Metro
Santutxu (L1, L2)
Vibe
Residential, authentic, market-focused Local Favorite

What to do: Santutxu Municipal Market (excellent fresh produce, less touristy than Ribera), local bars, Basque pelota fronton.

Key restaurants: Neighborhood bars with daily menus (menú del día), authentic pintxos.

Best for: Authentic market experience, local prices, residential Bilbao feel.

Deusto
Metro
Deusto (L1), Universidad (L1)
Vibe
University area, youthful, creative Local Favorite

What to do: University campus, Deusto Bridge, contemporary art galleries, student bars.

Key restaurants: Zarate (⭐ Michelin), university-area pintxos bars, budget-friendly menús.

Best for: Michelin seafood at Zarate, youthful vibe, lower prices.

Artxanda
Access
Funicular from Zazpi Kaleak
Vibe
Mountain views, traditional, peaceful Local Favorite

What to do: Panoramic city views, Artxanda Park, traditional asadores, sports complex.

Key restaurants: Txakoli Simón (asador), other traditional grills.

Best for: Sunset views, txuleta lunch, escape the city heat.

Martutene
Access
Bus/taxi from center (~15 min)
Vibe
Industrial/rural edge, authentic sidrería Local Favorite

What to do: Barkaiztegi sidrería/grill — the only cider house inside city limits.

Key restaurants: Barkaiztegi.

Best for: Cider house experience without leaving Bilbao.

Beyond Bilbao — Base Towns for Day Trips

Getxo
Metro
Line 1 (Algorta, Bidezabal, Ibarbengoa)
Vibe
Coastal, upscale residential, beaches Local Favorite

Why stay: Budget-friendly hotels, Metro Line 1 direct to Bilbao (25 min), Las Arenas beach, cliff walk to Old Port (Puerto Viejo), Puente Colgante (UNESCO transporter bridge).

Restaurants: Waterfront seafood, pintxos in Algorta Old Port.

Gernika-Local Areas
Bus
Bizkaibus A3247 / ALSA from Bilbao
Vibe
Coastal city, distinct neighborhoods Local Favorite

Gros: Surf vibe, Zurriola beach, modern pintxos, younger crowd.

Antiguo: Residential, Ondarreta beach, quieter, family-friendly.

Egia: Artsy, Tabakalera cultural center, alternative scene.

Avoid for authenticity: Parte Vieja (Old Town) — very touristy in August.
