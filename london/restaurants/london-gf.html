🏆 Top Picks by Area & Occasion

Soho — Top 3 Dedicated / Excellent GF

🥇 El Pollote
100% Dedicated GF & Dairy-Free

Address: Carnaby Street, Soho | Tube: Oxford Circus / Piccadilly Circus (3 min)

Specialty: Latin fried chicken — Guava Glaze, Dulce de Leche wings. 100% gluten-free & dairy-free kitchen. High-energy "sabor latino" atmosphere.

Price: ££ | Booking: Walk-in / reservation via app

🥈 Bubala Soho
GF-Excellent (mostly GF vegetarian)

Address: 15 Poland St, W1F 8PR | Tube: Oxford Circus (4 min)

Specialty: Middle Eastern vegetarian — halloumi, mezze, vegetable plates. No GF bread (crudités for dips). Very few dishes contain gluten.

Price: ££ | Hours: Mon–Sat late, Sun 10pm

🥉 Mowgli Charlotte Street
GF-Excellent (dedicated non-gluten menu)

Address: Charlotte St, Fitzrovia | Tube: Goodge St / Tottenham Court Rd (3 min)

Specialty: Indian street food — dedicated non-gluten menu. Treacle Tamarind fries, Goan fish curry, Temple Dahl. Fairy lights, swing seats.

Price: ££ | Booking: Yes

Bank / Financial District — Top 3

🥇 Browns Brasserie (Old Jewry)
Coeliac UK Accredited GF-Excellent

Address: Old Jewry, Bank | Tube: Bank (2 min)

Specialty: Sophisticated brasserie, full GF Afternoon Tea, all-day dining. Coeliac UK accredited for coeliac-safe confidence.

Price: ££–£££ | Best for: Business lunch, special occasions

🥈 Honest Burgers Bank
GF-Friendly (GF buns)

Address: Bank / Spitalfields locations | Tube: Bank (3 min)

Specialty: Reliable GF buns for all burgers, signature local burger per location. Simple, relaxed, consistent.

Price: ££ | Best for: Casual business lunch, solo dining

🥉 Caravan City / Pho Bank
GF-Friendly

Caravan: All-day dining, GF bread options. Pho: Coeliac UK accredited, separate Coeliac-safe menu.

Tube: Bank / Liverpool St / Cannon St — multiple locations

🍰 GF Afternoon Tea & Sunday Roast

🏆 Top GF Afternoon Tea Venues

  • Roast (Borough Market) — Dedicated GF menu, perched above market, "indoor garden" setting. Afternoon tea from £30pp.
  • Brigit's Bakery (Covent Garden) — Routemaster bus sightseeing tour + GF afternoon tea (ciabatta sandwiches, scones, carrot cake). Kids half-price Tuesdays in Aug 2026.
  • Browns Brasserie (Old Jewry, Bank) — Coeliac UK accredited, full GF afternoon tea, sophisticated setting.
  • Browns Brasserie (Butler's Wharf, London Bridge) — Same accreditation, riverside location.

🍖 Sunday Roast GF Options

  • Roast (Borough Market) — GF Sunday roast available, book ahead.
  • The Guinea Grill (Mayfair) — Traditional, ask for GF yorkshire puddings.
  • Hawksmoor (multiple) — Excellent GF awareness, dedicated fryer for chips.

🌽 Naturally Gluten-Free Cuisines (Corn-Based)

These venues use corn/plantain as staple — naturally GF with minimal cross-contamination risk:

Paladar (Waterloo)
100% Dedicated GF

Cuisine: Latin American wine bar & bodega | Tube: Waterloo (5 min)

Highlights: Blue corn tortillas, empanadas, green plantain crisps, pulled pork tacos, cinnamon churros. 100% GF kitchen. Sharing plates, great wine list.

Arepa & Co (Haggerston)
Naturally GF (corn-based)

Cuisine: Venezuelan café | Tube: Haggerston / Hoxton (5 min)

Highlights: Arepas (corn bread), cachapas (corn pancakes), rice bowls. Most menu naturally GF. Excellent weekend brunch.

Santo Remedio (London Bridge)
GF-Excellent

Cuisine: Mexican | Tube: London Bridge (2 min)

Highlights: Corn tortilla tacos (hibiscus, pork belly, soft shell crab), Michelin-recommended 2022. High % naturally GF.
