How to Get Tickets & Access

Practical tips for navigating London's gatekept underground scene.

🎫

Resident Advisor (RA)

Primary platform for warehouse/club events. Create account, enable notifications for favourite promoters. Tickets often sell out in minutes.

📱

Encrypted Location Reveals

For secret events (SIMMS, etc.), locations sent via encrypted email/Telegram 24–48hrs before. Check spam folder. Have phone charged for navigation.

💷

Cash & ID Ready

Many warehouse venues: cash-only bars, strict 18+/21+ ID checks. Bring physical ID (passport/driving licence) — photos on phone often rejected.

🕐

Arrive Early or On Time

Secret locations often have single entry points — queues form fast. For immersive dining/theatre: arrive 15–30 mins before stated time for check-in.

🔕

Respect No-Photo Policies

Strict at FOLD, SIMMS, Sauna Rave, most warehouse events. Phone cameras ruin the vibe and can get you ejected. Be present.

🚇

Plan Transport Home

Events end 3–6am. Night Tube (Fri/Sat) on Victoria, Jubilee, Central, Northern, Piccadilly lines. Uber/Bolt surge pricing — check buses (N-routes) or pre-book.
