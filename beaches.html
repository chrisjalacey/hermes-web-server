🏖️ Beach Guide — Basque Coast & Getaria

Beaches, coves, and coastal spots for mid-August visitors — facilities, transport & practical tips

🔑 Key Insights for Beach-Goers

  • August water temperature: 21–24°C — perfect for swimming; 3/2mm wetsuit for surf.
  • Getaria's Malkorbe Beach: Blue Flag, sandy, calm waters, full facilities, 5 min walk from town centre.
  • Zarautz (15 min away): 2.5–3 km of sand — longest Basque beach, surf capital, big parking but busy in August.
  • Flysch Coast (Zumaia/Deba): Dramatic vertical rock strata; low tide essential for access.
  • Parking in Getaria: Blue Zone regulated daily 10:00–20:00 (June–Sept); €1.50/hr, €12/day max.
  • Dogs: Prohibited during bathing hours; allowed 21:00–06:00 (Easter–Oct 14).

🌊 Beach Culture & August Tips

August Crowds: All beaches busy 11:00–19:00. Arrive before 10:30 for parking at Zarautz/Malkorbe. San Sebastián beaches extremely crowded — consider Getaria/Zarautz instead.
Typical Beach Hours
11:00–20:00 peak; Spanish families arrive 12:00–13:00, stay till sunset
Nudist Sections
Azkorri (Getxo), Saturraran (Mutriku), parts of Barinatxe; signposted
Best Times
Early morning (9:00–11:00) for space; late afternoon (18:00+) for cooler temps
Tide Awareness
Flysch (Zumaia/Deba) only accessible at low tide; check tables day before
