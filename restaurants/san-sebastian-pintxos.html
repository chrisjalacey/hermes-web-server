🗺️ Pintxos Crawl Strategy for Your Day Trip (Aug 15)

🎯 Optimized Route from Illunbe P+R (Bus Line 28 → "Centro")

  1. 09:50 Arrive "Centro" stop → 2 min walk to La Concha beach
  2. 10:00–11:30 Coffee & Gilda at Bar Sport (Plaza Constitución) or Café Bar Bilbao (Plaza Nueva) — early, before crowds
  3. 11:20–12:30 Queue for La Cuchara de San Telmo — arrive by 11:20 for 12:30 opening (queue forms early)
  4. 12:30–14:00 La Cuchara — Foie a la Plancha, caramelized pork cheeks
  5. 14:00–15:30 Pintxos crawl 31 de Agosto: Bar Martínez → Gandarias → La Viña (cheesecake)
  6. 15:30–17:00 Pescadería street: Bar Txepetxa (anchovies) → Bar Nestor (tortilla at 15:30 sharp!) → Casa Alcalde / Bar Baztan
  7. 17:00–19:30 Break / beach / Mont Urgull
  8. 19:30–22:00 Evening pintxos: Bar Martínez (revisit) → La Cuchara evening session (arrive 19:00) → Bar Txepetxa evening
  9. 22:30 Semana Grande Fireworks over La Concha
  10. 22:45 Walk to "Centro" bus stop → Line 28 back to Illunbe (runs until 23:30; check for festival extensions)

Pro tips: Arrive by 11:20 for La Cuchara (opens 12:30). Bar Nestor tortilla at 13:00 & 19:30 sharp — arrive 30 min early. Bar Txepetxa closed Mon. La Viña closed Mon. Carry cash (many bars prefer it). Pace yourself!
