🥩 Traditional Basque Grills & Sidrerías

Asadores and cider houses for txuleta, txotx ritual, and authentic Basque dining

Sidrería Season: Traditional txotx (cider from barrel) season runs mid-January to May. Petritegi and Barkaiztegi operate year-round; Zapiain is seasonal only. Call ahead to confirm txotx availability in August.
