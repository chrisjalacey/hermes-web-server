🚌 Day Trips from Bilbao

Non-touristy excursions with logistics, transport, and local insights

🌊 San Sebastián (Donostia) — Local Neighborhoods Focus

Skip the touristy Parte Vieja/La Concha circuit. Focus on neighborhoods where locals actually live and eat.

San Sebastián — Gros, Antiguo & Egia 🚌 Bus 1h15m / 🚗 1h5m
Transport
Bizkaibus A3247 (frequent) or ALSA; park at Illunbe P+R (free) + Dbus Line 28
Best Day
Aug 15 (Semana Grande finale + fireworks)
Time Needed
Full day (8:30–23:00)

🏄 Gros Neighborhood (Zurriola Beach)

  • Surf culture, younger crowd, modern pintxos bars
  • Zurriola beach — better for surfing/watching than swimming
  • Pintxos: La Cuchara de San Telmo (creative), Astelena (traditional)

🏖️ Antiguo Neighborhood (Ondarreta Beach)

  • Residential, quieter, families, beautiful Ondarreta beach
  • Peine del Viento (Comb of the Wind) sculptures by Chillida
  • Pintxos: A Fuego Negro (creative, reservation essential)

🎨 Egia Neighborhood

  • Artsy, alternative, Tabakalera cultural center (former tobacco factory)
  • Great views from Tabakalera rooftop
  • Pintxos: Egia Taberna, La Esquina
🎆 Aug 15 Special: Semana Grande finale with fireworks over La Concha bay at 22:30. Take Dbus Line 28 from "Centro" stop back to Illunbe P+R (runs until ~23:30, check festival extensions). National holiday — festive atmosphere, some shops closed.

🍷 Getaria — Txakoli, Grilled Fish & Coastal Charm

Getaria 🚗 35 min / 🚌 Bus 1h
Transport
Car recommended (coastal drive); Bizkaibus A3513 from Bilbao
Best For
Txakoli wineries, grilled turbot, medieval port
Time Needed
Half day (combine with Zarautz)

🍇 Must-Visit Wineries

  • Txomin Etxaniz — Family-run, parral elevated vine system, tours + tasting with anchovies
  • Astarbe — Sparkling Txakoli (Byhur), modern facility, guided tours
  • Gaintza — Organic, beautiful coastal setting

🐟 Grilled Fish Specialists

  • Elkano (⭐ Michelin) — World-famous grilled turbot, book months ahead
  • Kaia Kai — Harbor views, excellent grilled fish, more accessible
  • Boliño — Traditional, great value, local favorite

🏰 Medieval Port

  • Church of San Salvador (Gothic), narrow streets, fisherman's houses
  • Mouse of Getaria (ratón de Getaria) — distinctive mountain silhouette

🏰 Hondarribia — Medieval Border Town

Hondarribia / Fuenterrabía 🚗 1h10m / 🚌 Bus 1h30m
Transport
Car via AP-8 (toll) or N-1; bus from San Sebastián
Best For
Medieval walls, marina, txakoli, seafood
Time Needed
Half day
  • Casco Viejo: Perfectly preserved medieval quarter inside walls, colorful balconies
  • Marina district: Fishing port turned pintxo paradise — Hermandad de Pescadores, Arroka Berri
  • Castle of Charles V: Parador hotel, panoramic views
  • Txakoli: Local denomination (Hondarrabi Zuri), visit Bodega Txakoli Etxezuri

🌊 Bermeo & Mundaka — Fishing Port & Surf Capital

Bermeo & Mundaka 🚗 45 min / 🚌 Bus 1h15m
Transport
Car recommended (BI-631 coastal road); Bizkaibus A3517 from Bilbao
Best For
Urdaibai biosphere, surf, grilled fish, Gaztelugatxe views
Time Needed
Full day (combine with Cabo Matxitxako)

🎣 Bermeo

  • Active fishing port — most authentic in Basque Country
  • Ercilla Tower (fisherman's museum), old town, port
  • Restaurants: Kaia Kai, Boliño — harbor views, grilled fish, txakoli

🏄 Mundaka

  • World-famous left-hand river mouth wave
  • Charming port, Urdaibai bird reserve views
  • Restaurant: Portuondo (views of wave/mouth)

⛰️ Cabo Matxitxako (Combine!)

  • 5 min drive from Bermeo — Gaztelugatxe alternative (no reservation needed)
  • Two lighthouses (1852 & 1909), cliff-top Gaztelugatxe viewpoint, coastal loop trail
  • Free parking at Matxitxako lighthouse (~30 spaces, arrive early)

🏛️ Gernika-Lumo — History, Market & Symbolism

Gernika-Lumo 🚗 35 min / 🚌 Bus 45 min
Transport
Bizkaibus A3247 (frequent); train Euskotren E4 from Bilbao-Atxuri
Best Day
Monday — legendary weekly market Market Day
Time Needed
Half day
  • Monday Market: One of Basque Country's best — produce, cheese, talo (corn tortillas), crafts
  • Casa de Juntas & Tree of Gernika: Symbolic heart of Basque democracy
  • Peace Museum: Picasso's Guernica context, bombing history
  • Parque de los Pueblos de Europa: Sculpture park
  • Restaurants: Talo with chistorra at market, Zortziko for traditional

🍷 Rioja Alavesa — Small Family Bodegas (Not Big Tours)

Rioja Alavesa (Laguardia, Labastida, Elciego) 🚗 1h / 🚌 Bus 1h30m
Transport
Car essential for small bodegas; bus to Laguardia only
Best For
Artisan wineries, medieval towns, wine + food pairings
Time Needed
Full day

🏘️ Base: Laguardia

  • Medieval walled town, car-free center, underground wine cellars (cuevas)
  • Stay here for multi-day wine exploration

🍇 Small Bodegas to Visit (Book Ahead)

  • Bodega El Fabulista (Laguardia) — "Subway winery," unique underground tour
  • Bodegas Artadi — Terroir-focused, single-vineyard wines
  • Bodegas Más que Vinos — Natural wines, young producers
  • Bodegas Baigorri — Gravity-flow, stunning architecture

Avoid: Large commercial tours (Marqués de Riscal, Ysios) unless you want the "Disney" experience.

🏞️ Gorbeia & Urkiola Natural Parks — Hiking Locals Do

Gorbeia / Urkiola 🚗 30–45 min
Transport
Car required
Best For
Hiking, nature, sanctuary views, cheese
Time Needed
Half to full day

🏔️ Gorbeia Natural Park

  • Highest peak in Bizkaia (1,482m) — cross at summit
  • Beech forests, waterfalls (Guajuluri), wild horses (pottokas)
  • Garena (⭐ Michelin) in Dima at park edge — 16-course farmhouse tasting

⛪ Urkiola Natural Park & Sanctuary

  • Sanctuary of Urkiola (Santuario de Urkiola) — dramatic limestone peaks
  • Anboto peak (1,331m) — Basque mythology (Mari, goddess)
  • Idiazabal cheese producers nearby — visit Ondarre or Arangio

🏘️ Balmaseda & Orduña — Medieval Inland Towns

Balmaseda & Orduña 🚗 30–40 min / 🚂 Train (Balmaseda)
Transport
Cercanías C4 from Bilbao-Abando (35 min to Balmaseda); car for both
Best For
Medieval bridges, historic quarters, quiet atmosphere
Time Needed
Half day each

🌉 Balmaseda

  • Medieval bridge (Puente Viejo), old town, Church of San Severino
  • La Encartada beret factory museum (boinas)
  • River Kadagua walk

🏛️ Orduña

  • "Little Venice" — canal through town center
  • Sanctuary of La Antigua (Orduña), Urkabustaiz reservoir
  • Gateway to Gorbeia/Urkiola from Álava side
